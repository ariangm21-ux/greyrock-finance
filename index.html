A letter from the partners

To those who have been introduced,

This page is not an advertisement. We don't run campaigns, keep a public client list or publish our returns. It is here as a courtesy, for people who have heard our name from someone they trust.

Families in Bangladesh have built remarkable things in a single generation. Our work is to make sure what you have built lasts for the next three: protected, patiently grown and handed on well.

So we keep our circle small. Every family works with a partner, not an account manager. We say no more often than we say yes, and we invest our own capital alongside yours.

If that is what you have been looking for, we would be glad to meet.

With respect,The PartnersGreyrock Finance · Dhaka

Philosophy

Four principles we do not bend.

  1. I

    Few, by design

    We limit how many families we serve, so each one has a partner's full attention, not a call centre's.

  2. II

    Patience over noise

    We invest for decades, not quarters. We would rather miss a fashion than risk a fortune.

  3. III

    Discretion is the service

    No logos on our door and no names in the press. Who you are and what you own is never ours to share.

  4. IV

    Our capital beside yours

    The partners invest alongside our families, on the same terms. When you do well, so do we, and not before.

Approach

How a relationship begins.

  1. 01

    An introduction

    From a client, a partner or an adviser you trust. It is the only way we take on new families.

  2. 02

    A private conversation

    At our office or in your home. We listen first: your family, your business, what you want to protect.

  3. 03

    A written mandate

    Your goals, your limits and what we will never do are put on paper before any capital moves.

  4. 04

    One partner, for life

    The same senior partner, reachable directly, through every market and every generation.
